## Interview Tips

### How to Approach This Question

1. Clarify what's being asked
2. Provide context from your experience
3. Explain your approach
4. Discuss trade-offs
5. Mention scalability/reliability
6. Ask clarifying questions if needed

### Strong Indicators

✓ Asking clarifying questions  
✓ Providing specific examples  
✓ Discussing trade-offs  
✓ Mentioning scale/performance  
✓ Referencing real projects  
✓ Showing business awareness  

### Red Flags to Avoid

✗ Generic textbook answers  
✗ No practical examples  
✗ Ignoring trade-offs  
✗ Overconfidence in weak areas  
✗ No business context  
✗ Cannot explain "why"
