## Storytelling Tips

### Story Structure

1. ✓ Start with context (5-10 seconds)
2. ✓ Explain the challenge (10-15 seconds)
3. ✓ Focus on YOUR actions (20-30 seconds)
4. ✓ Quantify the results (10-15 seconds)
5. ✓ Explain what you learned (5-10 seconds)

**Total story duration**: 60-90 seconds

### Telling It Effectively

✓ Use specific numbers and metrics  
✓ Show, don't tell (specific actions, not "I'm good at leadership")  
✓ Connect to the job requirement  
✓ Be humble but confident  
✓ Make it memorable  

### What NOT to Do

✗ "We did..." (say "I did")  
✗ "It was probably..." (be specific)  
✗ Make it too long (keep it under 2 minutes)  
✗ Blame others  
✗ Exaggerate  
✗ Make it vague  

## Preparation

### Practice Tips

- [ ] Tell this story out loud 5+ times
- [ ] Record yourself telling it
- [ ] Time yourself (aim for 60-90 seconds)
- [ ] Prepare 2-3 variations (emphasize different aspects)
- [ ] Prepare answers for potential follow-ups
- [ ] Practice under pressure (time limits)

### Readiness Checklist

- [ ] Story is specific and concrete
- [ ] Numbers/metrics are accurate
- [ ] Focus is on YOUR actions
- [ ] Result is quantified
- [ ] You can tell it under 90 seconds
- [ ] You're prepared for follow-ups
